Here we are, come to the end of 2021, and I thought it would be a good time to make some New Year’s resolutions for 2022.

<!--more-->

I am not normally one for making resolutions like this, but I am trying to establish a New Normal - as is the whole world - and one of the things I most want to do is be more intentional in my life.  Some of these are more realistic than others, but I believe in all of them.  I present them in order from most realistic to least realistic in my completely subjective opinion.

### 1. Write a blog entry every two weeks

I feel that the practice of writing is important.  I don’t really think anyone is going to read what I write.  I don’t look at the hits on this website, and I think that is healthy for me.  But I need to be in better practice making structured arguments and thoughtful, introspective prose.  No better way to do so than here.

{{< figure src="../../assets/images/writing.png" title="The value of writing" >}}

### 2. Daily Exercise

I need to be better about systematic exercise.  I plan on figuring out a 20-30 minute routine I can do in my office in the morning, and being better about taking walks.  

### 3. Weekly Praise

Gratitude and appreciation are important things.  I work with a number of excellent people, and I have some fantastic humans in my life.  I don’t think that there is enough praise for people.  I might do so on social media, in a meeting, or in person - but I think adding to the positivity of the world is worth it.  When, and to whom, I praise I’ll write on my calendar to keep myself accountable.

### 4. UTC or GTFO 

I believe it is a worthwhile experiment to try moving entirely to UTC and not observe daylight savings time anymore.  I would see things shift around me, but the items on my schedule that I control would stay fixed.  Can I do it?  Only time will tell (ha ha ha).

### 5. Lose 15 pounds

Who doesn’t want to lose weight?

Happy New Year to all!
