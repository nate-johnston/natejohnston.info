#### Memory
I have been pondering the topic of memory lately.  One of the defining things about "being me" is my memory - or lack thereof.  I have some strong, clear memories - highlights of my marriage and my daughter's life - but there are a lot of details about daily life that I forget.  I don't want to sound like I am disabled in some way by this, but not managing the situation leads to some Very Bad Outcomes.

Those memories that I have trouble with, are as though I see them through a hazy fog, with their outlines and features indistinct.  Sometimes, my family will say "why did you change how you do things, and start doing differently what you have always done a certain way?"  The answer is, I don't have a clue how I have always done things.  Much of the time I simply don't remember how I did it before.  

#### Adaptation
So how do I maintain my ability to act as a high-functioning adult?  I have tried many methods of mnemonic memorization or memory enhancement over the years.  I am particularly facinated with the ["memory palace"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Method_of_loci) concept.  Despite the fact that I am very much a visual thinker, it has never worked at all for me.

So what I have done is externalize my memory as much as possible.  At first, it was scraps of paper.  Then it was todo lists in notepads and composition notebooks.  At one point I set up a special email address that I would email my todo items to.  But what happened was that they all failed with the same issue: I would start all gung-ho about them, but then would lose steam after three to six months and the system would fail.  

The system I have been using currently has been serving me well for a full year, and I am going strong with it.  This being perhaps the longest streak I have had with a single system, I thought I would write about it.

#### Conclusion
All of these allow me to curate the information I have available to me in my moment of forgetfulness, and remove clutter from my external memory.  I hope that they help someone else out there, but since I don't really write for an audience beyond myself, mostly I hope that later, when I have forgotten some part of this, I can come back and remind myself.
