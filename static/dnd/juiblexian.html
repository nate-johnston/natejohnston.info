Race: Juiblexian
  • Base speed: 30'
  • Ability Score Increases: +2 to CON
  • Age: A typical Juiblexian is between two and one hundred years old. The maximum lifespan of one remains elusive and often inconclusive, most Juiblexians show no sign of deterioration due to age.
  • Alignment: Most oozes are neutral, though there are some that develop their complex system of morals based on the beings they have consumed across their existence. Some scholars believe their spiritual essence colors a Juiblexian's moral demeanor.
  • Size: Your size is Medium. You stand between 5 and 6 1/2 feet tall. Weight and build are affexted by their subrace and aesthetical choice.
  • Speed: Your base walking speed is 30'.
  • Amorphous Ooze: Your ooze nature is represented in the following benefits:
    • You can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch wide without squeezing.
    • Due to the lack of a true physical structure, a Juiblexian has advantage on saving throws against being blinded, deafened, exhauston, or knocked prone.
    • You are immune to poison and the poisoned condition.
    • You don't need sleep and don't suffer the effects of exhaustion due to lack of rest, and magic can't put you to sleep.
  • Blind Vision: While in humanoid form, you can appear to have eyes. They do not function like other races. Instead, you have Blindsight up to 60 feet, using ambient sound and via consumption of air particles to smell their quarry. Beyond this range, you are blind and cannor percieve beyond it.
  • Gelatinous Trance: When you take a long rest, you must spend at least 6 hours in an inactive, motionless state, rather than sleeping. In this state, you appear inert, but it doesn't render you unconscious and you can see and hear as normal.
  • Languages: Common, Abyssal, and one language of your choice.
  • Quirks: I took 6 of the Juiblexian quirks that I could see using; roll 1d6 when in a situation to be quirky.
    • [1] You don't understand emotions and often misread emotional cues.
    • [2] You always feel hungry and often start grabbing the nearest edible source of nourishment.
    • [3] You learned about the world by devouring it, you sometimes take objects and ingest them to learn about it.
    • [4] You always have an anacedotal tale about your times in your original ooze form.
    • [5] You find talking tiresome and sometimes revert back to talking using vibrations through your ooze-like surface.
    • [6] Your are curious about the rituals of the “solids” and obsess on learning about them.
See: The Juiblexian: Slimes Just Want to Adventure.

Subrace: Mnemonic
Racial Traits
  • Ability Score Increase: +1 to Int
  • False Appearance: As an action, you can transform your appearance. You decide what you look like, including your height, weight, facial features, the sound of your voice, coloration, hair length, sex, and any other distinguishing characteristics. You can make yourself appear as a member of another race, although none of your game statistics change. You also can't appear as a creature a different size than you, and your basic shape stays the same: if you're bipedal, you can't use this trait to become quadrupedal, for instance. Your clothing and other equipment don't change in appearance, size, or shape to match your new form, requiring you to keep a few extra outfits on hand to make the most compelling disguise possible.

    Even to most observers, the ruse is usually indiscernable. If you arouse suspicion, or if a wary creature suspects something is amiss, you have advantage on any Charisma (Deception) check you make to avoid detection.

    You can also assume the appearance of any creature you have consumed through your Mnemonic Echoes feature. You can remember a number of appearances equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum 1).

  • Mnemonic Echoes: The myriad of dissonant voices echoes within your being, granting you knowledge not your own. You gain skill proficiecy of your choice, one tool proficiency of your choice, and fluency in one language of your choice. Whenever a humanoid creature dies within 5 feet of you, you can spend your reaction to consume the target. Once consumed, after your next long rest, you may change either your skill proficiency, tool proficiency, or language fluency to one of the proficiencies or languages of the creature.
Optional Racial Feats
  • Morphic Body: You do not possess the normal anatomy of a solid being, allowing you to shift or move vital organs out of harm's way. You gain the following benefits: Increase your Constitution score by 1, to a maximum of 20. Whenever you are hit by an attack that is a critical hit, you can use your reaction to turn the triggering attack to a normal hit.
  • Haunting Mimicry: When you assume a form of a consumed humanoid, you also devour their thoughts and memories. You gain the following benefits:
    • Increase your Intelligence or Charisma score by 1, to a maximum 20.
    • Whenever you assume an appearance of a creature consumed by your Mnemonic Echoes feature, you gain access to all information that the humanoid would freely share with a casual acquaintance. Such information includes general details on its background and personal life but doesn’t include secrets. The information is enough that you can pass yourself off as the person by drawing on its memories.
    • Your uncanny portrayal of the Mnemonic Echoes strikes fear to even those who knew them in life. If you speak to a humanoid for at least 1 minute, you can attempt to spark paranoia in their mind. At the end of the conversation, you make a Charisma (Deception) check contested against the target’s Wisdom (Insight) check. On a success, the target is frightened of you for 1 hour, unless it is attacked or damaged, or until it witnesses its allies being attacked or damaged. If the target succeeds on its saving throw, the target has no hint that you tried to frighten it. Once you use this ability, you can’t do so again until after you finish a short or long rest.
