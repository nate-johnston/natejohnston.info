Journal

Session 2: May 23, 2019
  • We descended the stairs from the top floor to the basement, where we found many crypts.
  • One crypt had a coffin that had a swarm of bugs, which we killed.
  • After that there was a large area after which Ucalegon was ambushed by a grick and almost killed.
  • After a maze of tunnels, we found a bedroom, where an undead Mrs Durst crashed through a painting of herself to ambush us.
  • On a lower level we found an area with a gallery of evil artifacts, and a portcullis to a room that was partially submerged.
  • We located a prison area with corpses inside. We also found an idiot who had been trapped there as well.
  • There was a gap in the wall that allowed us to get into the partially submerged room, which had a bloodstained sacrificial altar inside. A shambling mound with Mr. Durst's face broke through a wall and attacked. Ucalegon, Tanad, and the idiot were able to get the portcullis up and everyone escaped but poor Delma, who was eaten by the mound.
  • We ran out of the house, which was quaking and shivering with malevolent intent towards us. When we got out, Strahd was there in a coach giving us the golf clap treatment. He gave us a letter inviting us to dinner at Castle Ravenloft, and to present the seal at the inn and mercantile to "receive better services than is normally offered to strangers". It also included a less-than-veiled threat to stay out of his business.
  • Loot: Pipes of haunting, Cloak of Protection +1, and ring of mind shielding, and about 200 gp of magical components
Session 1: March 29, 2019
  • We started at the Hammered Basilisk Inn. We socialized until a stranger came and bade us go on a quest to help someone for great riches and rewards.
  • Once we set out on our journey, we were surrounded by supernatural fog.
  • We fought some dire wolves.
  • We came to huge gates with headless guardian statues.
  • We came to a town. In the town, two creepy children bade us help them; their father had gone to fight a monster in the basement. The house belonged to a family named the "Dursts"; logo is a golden windmill on a red background.
  • We fought our way through a demented posessed doll, an animated suit of armor, and a ghost-like being with a banshee's shriek. There was also a ghostly dance party that Anemone experienced, and Count Von Strahd demonstrated his BDE by coming on to Anemone through the dream link.
